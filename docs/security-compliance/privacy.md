# Privacy

Thesis files, messages, grades, profiles, coordinator reports, similarity reports, and defense minutes are confidential academic records. Collect only data required for the workflow, separate profile and academic data where practical, restrict access by role and ownership, protect data in transit and at rest, and apply institution-approved retention and deletion policies. The privacy controls are target requirements until the application and storage paths are implemented and reviewed.

## Data classes

| Class | Examples | Access |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Name, institutional email, study group | Authorized roles only |
| Academic | Thesis drafts, grades, minutes | Student + assigned staff |
| Communication | Messages, attachments | Conversation members |
| Operational | Audit events, backups | Administrators, audited |

## Retention

Retention periods and deletion rules are set with the institution. Records required for academic appeals and audit are preserved; others are removed under the approved policy.
