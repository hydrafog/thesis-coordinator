# Team responsibilities

Divide implementation by the three PDF-defined work packages:

- **Cases 1–3** cover authentication, email, profiles, coordinator research areas and 5–7 student quotas, topic proposals, approval, assignment PDF generation, milestones, and deadline reminders.
- **Cases 4–6** cover the real-time server, private messaging, authorized MinIO uploads and downloads, versioned draft submissions, chapter reviews, and local similarity checking.
- **Cases 7–9** cover schema, CI/CD, deployment, committee and 20-minute slot scheduling, defense dossier verification, live grading, and minutes PDF generation.

Shared infrastructure, state transitions, authorization, audit events, and schema changes require coordinated review by every affected work package.
